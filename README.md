# Arnas Pajaujis

I build small, practical software — mostly for my own work, and for small businesses that need something specific rather than something general. Everything below runs as a fully automated agent system: agents that keep memory between runs and work under multiple layers of explicit rules and logic constraints, rather than being left to improvise.

## What I work on

**Knowledge tools**
Fully automated, with memory-backed agents and multiple rule layers governing what may and may not be concluded. Finds the right material in the first place — lectures, studies, books, long transcripts — and carries it through to something usable: broken into steps, matched to a situation, and turned into a plan that can be followed.

**Email**
A fully automated agent chain with persistent memory of every correspondent and every decision made so far, held in line by multiple rule sets. Handles the inbox end to end: understands who is writing and what they respond to, replies in the voice of the business, and keeps the whole thing in order without supervision.

**Financial markets**
Automated monitoring agents with long-term memory, constrained by multiple rule sets written down before they are used. Covers macro indicators, portfolio supervision with early warnings, and a search for opportunities that fit defined criteria.

**Statistics and live data**
Automated collection and assessment agents that remember the full history of what they have seen. They watch slow-moving situations no one has time to watch and report what changed and why it matters.

**Research, search and comparison**
Automated multi-agent search with memory across runs, bounded by criteria set in advance. Works through large amounts of material and narrows real options down to the few that decide the question.

**Profiling from text**
Automated analysis agents operating under strict limits on what may be inferred. Reads communication patterns out of written material: how someone argues, what they avoid, what they respond to. Built for negotiation preparation and difficult conversations.

**Personal systems**
Automated planning agents with memory of previous decisions and the constraints already agreed. Household finance planning written so it can be followed without a finance background, and a wider structure running from self-assessment through the first concrete steps to carrying a plan through.

Most of my work is in private repositories. Happy to talk through any of it on request.
